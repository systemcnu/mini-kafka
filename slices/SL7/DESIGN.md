| D-SL7-1 | **`cmd/showcase` — one process, client-surface-only (DD-23's shape).** Hosts the broker in-process exactly like demo/bench: `broker.New(broker.Config{Addr: "127.0.0.1:0", DataDir: <fresh os.MkdirTemp dir>})` — the loopback literal is HARD-CODED, no flag or env can rebind it (SHOW-3 by construction); the ephemeral port means there is no well-known broker port even on loopback. Self-feeder per DD-23: one `client.Producer` writing `msg-<n>` round-robin across 1 topic × 4 partitions at ~2 msg/s; one `client.GroupConsumer` polling and committing per batch (the headline machinery visibly alive — its `Assignment()` feeds the page); a ring buffer keeps the last 50 consumed records for the feed. Everything through the public `client` surface — frontiers derived from consumed offsets, counters from the feeder itself, diskBytes from `filepath.WalkDir` over the process's own data dir (checked every 30 s). **Concurrency contract (seat B's HIGH):** the feeder/consumer/walker goroutines NEVER share mutable state with HTTP handlers — they build a fresh immutable snapshot struct and swap it via ONE `atomic.Value` (or a single mutex copy-out); `/feed` handlers only ever read the last complete snapshot.
